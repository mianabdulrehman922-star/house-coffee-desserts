Craft & Passion

A Sanctuary for Coffee Lovers in Gulberg

Located at Plot 1, Block C1 in Gulberg III, HOUSE Coffee & Desserts was created with a clear vision: to present an elevated specialty coffee and dessert experience for Lahore's discerning coffee culture.

From carefully calibrated double shots of rich espresso to silky signature lattes, velvet matcha teas, and fresh slice-by-slice cheesecakes, every offering is served with meticulous attention to temperature, taste, and presentation.
